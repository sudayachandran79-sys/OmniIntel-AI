# Intelligent Information Understanding & Processing

## EMPATHY & USER NEEDS

### Information Users
- Find relevant information without reviewing everything manually.
- Understand information quickly and clearly.
- Identify important details that require attention.
- Compare information from different sources.
- Avoid missing useful or critical information.
- Use information with greater confidence.

### Operations & Administrative Teams
- Handle large volumes of information more efficiently.
- Reduce repetitive review and organization work.
- Find required information without unnecessary searching.
- Identify important, incomplete, or conflicting information.
- Reduce delays caused by manual information handling.
- Maintain more consistent understanding across information sources.

## Common Pain Points
- Large volumes of information.
- Different formats and structures.
- Information distributed across multiple sources.
- Repetitive manual review.
- Difficulty finding and comparing relevant information.
- Important details being overlooked.
- Delays caused by manual processing.
- Repeated effort and inconsistent understanding.
